# ALT — User Guide

## Getting Started
1. Open the app and click **Create Account** on the landing page.
2. Fill in your name, email, and a password (must include an uppercase
   letter, lowercase letter, digit, and special character).
3. Log in with your new account.

## Translating Text
Go to **Translate**, choose a source language (or leave it on
**Auto-detect**), choose a target language, type or paste your text, and
click **Translate**. You can swap languages with the ⇄ button and download
the result.

## Translating a Document
Go to **Document Translation**, upload a TXT/DOCX/PDF/CSV/JSON/MD file
(up to 20MB by default), choose your languages, and click **Translate
Document**. Long documents are automatically split into chunks and
reassembled.

## Translating an Image (OCR)
Go to **OCR Translation**, upload a photo or screenshot containing text,
and click **Extract & Translate**. Works best with clear, well-lit text.

## Voice Translation
Go to **Voice Translation**, upload an audio file of spoken words, choose
the spoken language and target language, and click **Transcribe &
Translate**. Enable "Read translation aloud" to also get an audio version
of the translation (not available for every language).

## History & Favorites
**History** shows everything you've translated. Use the search box and
language filters to find something specific, star (☆) an item to favorite
it, and export your results as TXT, CSV, JSON, DOCX, or PDF.

## Settings
Under **Settings** you can set your default languages, switch themes,
turn on accessibility options (high contrast, larger text, reduced
motion), check whether the translation model is available offline, and
download a full export of your own data.

## For Administrators
Admins see an additional **Admin Panel** link with tabs for system
overview, user management (search, promote/demote, enable/disable,
delete), activity logs, and live system health.
